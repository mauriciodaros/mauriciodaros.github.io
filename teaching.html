Stochastic Processes

Instructors

Mario Alejandro Munoz (only until August 26th) and me.

If you have any concerns or questions, please reach out to me at mauricio.andrade@fgv.br

Schedule and Classroom

Mondays and Wednesdays, 16:20-18:00. Auditorium 308.

  • First exam on October 7; second exam on November 30.
  • There will be no class on September 7, October 12, or November 2.

    • Syllabus

    The course will follow closely the stochastic processes lecture notes of Yuri Saporito.

    • Discrete-time Markov chains.
    • Continuous-time Markov chains.
    • Poisson process.
    • Birth and death process.
    • Queueing theory.
    • Renewal process.
    • Discrete-time martingales.

      • Bibliography

      • Yuri Saporito's lecture notes on stochastic processes.
      • Grimmett, G. R. and Stirzaker, D. R. (2001). Probability and Random Processes. Oxford.
      • Steele, J. M. (2012). Stochastic Calculus and Financial Applications. Springer.
      • Hoel, P., Port, S. and Stone, C. (1986). Introduction to Stochastic Processes. Waveland.
      • Berestycki, N. and Sousi, P. (2017). Applied Probability.

      Grading

      • Assessment consists of two exams, P1 and P2, with final grade M = (P1 + P2)/2.
