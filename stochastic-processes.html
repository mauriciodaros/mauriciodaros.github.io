Syllabus

This course follows the stochastic processes lecture notes of Yuri Saporito.

  • Discrete-time Markov chains.
  • Discrete-time martingales.
  • Continuous-time Markov chains.
  • Brownian motion.
  • Stochastic Calculus

Bibliography

  • [YS] Yuri Saporito's lecture notes on stochastic processes.
  • [GS] Grimmett, G. R. and Stirzaker, D. R. (2001). Probability and Random Processes. Oxford.
  • [S] Steele, J. M. (2012). Stochastic Calculus and Financial Applications. Springer.
  • [HPS] Hoel, P., Port, S. and Stone, C. (1986). Introduction to Stochastic Processes. Waveland.
  • [BS] Berestycki, N. and Sousi, P. (2017). Applied Probability.
